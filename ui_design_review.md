### 🔹 Style 1: Frosted Glassmorphism (*Linear / Vercel Standard*)
- **Aesthetic**: Translucent obsidian cards with background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), subtle 1px border highlights, and cyan/emerald glowing indicators.
- **Best For**: Modern tech SaaS, developer portals, and high-tech admin tools.
